# Code of conduct

We want mealie-mcp-server to be a welcoming place to contribute, ask questions,
and learn, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience. Assume good intent and ask for context.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback calmly.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the time of volunteer contributors.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
publication of private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to [the project maintainer, @brunofelixns](https://github.com/brunofelixns).
Use a private contact method listed on that profile when available. If no private
method is listed, open an issue asking for a private contact **without naming
people or describing the incident publicly**. Do not post personal information
or sensitive evidence in issues. GitHub's own abuse reporting tools are also
available for behavior on its platform.

The maintainer will review reports, protect reporters' privacy as far as possible,
and respond proportionately. Responses may include a warning, removal of content,
temporary restrictions, or a ban. Contributors asked to stop unacceptable
behavior are expected to comply. Decisions may be reconsidered when new
information is provided through the same contact route.
